Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Plankbury: Bridge Builder ("the game") is made by Saikaizen Innovations. This policy explains what data the game and the services inside it collect. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and we do not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, stars, bridge designs and settings are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Services we use

  • Google AdMob (with Meta Audience Network and Liftoff Monetize as bidding partners) shows ads. It may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland and in US states with privacy laws, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy options. Players who buy Pro see no ads. How Google uses data from ads.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) records anonymous gameplay events (for example level started, level finished, test runs) with an app instance ID, device model, OS version and country, to help us find hard or broken levels.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) sends crash reports (stack trace, device model, OS version) so we can fix crashes.
  • Google Play Billing handles the one-time Pro purchase. We never see your payment details.
  • Google Play Games Services (optional) shows leaderboards if you are signed in to Play Games; it uses your Play Games profile name and score.
  • Google Play In-App Review and In-App Updates show the Play rating prompt and update prompts.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules at most one local reminder a day on your device. No data leaves your device for this.

Children

The game is meant for players 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings, change consent in Settings > Privacy options, turn off notifications in Settings, and delete all game data by uninstalling. For analytics data deletion requests, write to us.
